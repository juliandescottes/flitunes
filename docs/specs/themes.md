# Themes

## Purpose

Tint the iTunes 10 look in another color.

## Behavior

- `themes.menu` The swatch button at the bottom of the source list opens
  the theme menu: Pink (the default), Silver, Mint Green, Fresh Blue and
  Lavender, the current one checked. Picking one tints the whole window;
  the button's tooltip names the theme.
- `themes.remembered` The theme is remembered in this browser.
- `themes.window` The installed app's title bar and the page's icon take
  the theme's tint too.

## UI and shortcuts

- The swatch is the last button at the bottom of the source list.
